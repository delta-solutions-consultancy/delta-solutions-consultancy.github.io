# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-13

Delta Solutions Consultancy is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle personal data in connection with this website, this repository, and any associated apps or projects.

1. No personal data collection by default
- We do not collect, store, or share personal data from users of this website, this repository, or any of our apps or projects, unless we explicitly state otherwise (for example, if a feature or form requests information and clearly explains its use).
- No personal information is required to view or use the public content in this repository or the associated site.

2. Analytics and tracking
- By default, we do not use third-party analytics, tracking pixels, or advertising networks in our projects or on this site.
- If analytics are added in the future, we will update this policy and disclose what is collected and how it is used.

3. Third-party services and links
- Our site or projects may contain links to third-party services or integrate third-party features. Those services may collect data according to their own privacy policies. We are not responsible for third-party practices and recommend you review their privacy policies before using them.

4. User-submitted content
- Any personal data you choose to submit (for example, in an issue, pull request, or contact form) may become public if posted in the repository or comments. Do not post sensitive personal information in public areas.

5. Children
- Our services are not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Data security
- We take reasonable measures to protect data in our control, but we cannot guarantee absolute security. If you have concerns about specific data, contact us.

7. Changes to this policy
- We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted here with an updated "Last updated" date.

8. Contact
- For questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us via the repository (open an issue or pull request).

This Privacy Policy applies to delta-solutions-consultancy/delta-solutions-consultancy.github.io and any other apps or projects under Delta Solutions Consultancy unless a project provides an alternate policy.
